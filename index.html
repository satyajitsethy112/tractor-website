About Us

Based in Ghuturu, Keonjhar, Odisha, Ramesh Chandra Sethy Tractors has established itself as a highly respected and trusted name in the local farming and construction community.

Our core mission is to provide timely, highly reliable, and efficient tractor services to the farmers and builders of Keonjhar. We firmly believe in hard work, honesty, and building long-lasting relationships with our clients.

Whether you require a field to be ploughed for the upcoming harvest season, or heavy materials delivered directly to your construction site, our dedicated team and fleet are always ready to deliver exceptional results.
